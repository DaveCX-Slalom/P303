# Project Brief (summary)

**Product:** Loyalty Pocket, a mobile-first web app for in-store loyalty members.
**User:** Priya, in an aisle, one hand on a cart, glancing at her phone.
**Job to do:** Show the member code, best offers right now, and what points can get her, in under 10 seconds.
**Core flows:** Pocket (code and points), Offers (clip/unclip), Stores (locator, aisle sections), History (points timeline and redeem).
**Constraints:** invented data, no client or Slalom info, Vue, responsive web (not native), password-protected deploy.
**Success:** passes Works / Repo / Design-and-thinking review. See `/BRIEF.md` for full detail.
